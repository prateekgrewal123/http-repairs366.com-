Privacy Policy

How Repair366 and Repair366 LLC collect, process, safeguard, and respect your personal information when visiting repairs366.com.

Effective Date / Last Updated: September 2026

Privacy Commitment Summary

At Repair366 (repairs366.com), operated by Repair366 LLC, we prioritize the privacy of our website visitors. We are an independent educational publication platform. We do not sell personal user data, we do not handle financial payment details through the informational publication, and we aim to maintain transparent practices regarding advertising cookies, analytics, and applicable privacy rights.

1. Overview & Operational Scope

This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Repair366 (accessible via repairs366.com) and how our operating business entity, Repair366 LLC, handles that data.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us via email at support@repairs366.com.

2. Information We Collect & How It Is Collected

Repair366 collects information to provide better educational content, improve site responsiveness, and maintain server security. Information is gathered through standard web infrastructure in the following ways:

A. Standard Log Files

Repair366 follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. Hosting companies commonly process this information as part of hosting and security services. The information collected by log files may include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. Such technical information is generally not directly linked by us to information that personally identifies a visitor.

B. Direct Email Communications

If you contact us directly via email (support@repairs366.com), we may receive additional information about you such as your name, email address, phone number, the contents of the message and/or attachments you may send us, and any other information you choose to provide.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like many websites, Repair366 may use cookies or similar technologies. These technologies can store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information may be used to optimize the user experience based on browser type and other technical information.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense Disclosures

Third-party ad servers or ad networks may use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in advertisements and links that appear on Repair366. These technologies may allow third parties to receive technical information such as your IP address and to measure advertising effectiveness or personalize advertising content.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie Notice

Google may use cookies, including DART-related technologies, to serve ads based upon visits to this and other sites. Visitors can review Google's advertising and privacy information at Google Ads & Privacy Technologies. Repair366 has no access to or control over cookies used by independent third-party advertisers. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options.

5. How We Use Collected Information

Repair366 LLC uses information collected on Repair366 for various operational purposes:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website infrastructure and technical uptime.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our educational articles and maintenance guides.
  • Understand and analyze how visitors interact with our repair and troubleshooting guides.
  • Develop new guides, features, and educational interactive tools.
  • Prevent fraudulent activities, spam, and technical site abuse.

6. Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or other processing of personal information.

The Right to Access & Rectification
You may have the right to request copies of your personal data and request correction of information you believe is inaccurate.

The Right to Erasure (Be Forgotten)
You may have the right to request deletion of your personal data under certain conditions.

CCPA Right: Do Not Sell My Data
Where applicable, California consumers may have the right to request that a business not sell their personal data. Repair366 does not sell user data.

The Right to Restrict Processing
Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request restriction of or object to certain processing.

If you would like to exercise applicable privacy rights, please contact us at support@repairs366.com. We will review and respond to valid requests within the period required by applicable law.

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide children's online activity. Repair366 does not knowingly collect Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that a child provided this kind of information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will take reasonable steps to review and remove such information where appropriate.
