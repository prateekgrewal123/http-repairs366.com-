How to Troubleshoot a Printer Paper Jam Safely

Learn a careful sequence for locating and clearing common paper jams without unnecessarily damaging the printer.

Educational note: This article provides general information. Always consult the official documentation for your specific model before performing maintenance or repair work.

1. Turn the Printer Off

Follow the manufacturer’s instructions for stopping the print job and powering down. Allow hot components to cool when the documentation recommends it.

2. Check the Paper Path

Open only the access areas identified in the printer manual. Look for loose or folded sheets in the input tray, output area, rear access, and other approved access points.

3. Remove Paper Gently

Pull paper slowly in the direction of the normal paper path whenever possible. Avoid sudden force, twisting, or tools that could scratch rollers or leave fragments inside the mechanism.

4. Inspect for Small Fragments

A torn sheet can leave small pieces behind. Check the accessible paper path carefully and make sure no visible scraps remain before closing the access panels.

5. Check the Tray

Remove excess paper and make sure the guides sit against the paper stack without squeezing it. Use paper that matches the device’s recommended size and type.

6. Restart and Test

Close every panel securely, power the device back on, and allow it to initialize. Run a small test job rather than immediately sending a large print queue.

7. Prevent Repeat Jams

Keep paper dry and flat, avoid overfilling trays, use the correct paper settings, and follow the manufacturer’s cleaning and roller-maintenance instructions.
